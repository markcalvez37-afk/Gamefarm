The project

Get to know GameFarm.

The proposed system addresses the difficulty of manually handling feeding, health care, breeding, conditioning, incubation, growth monitoring, and farm maintenance.

Our purpose

Organize, schedule, assign, and monitor farm activities in one place.

GameFarm provides a centralized platform that helps users identify what needs to be done, when it should be done, and who is assigned to perform the task.

See system features ↗

Implementation: The proposed system is intended for the SM (Salvador Montilla) GameFarm in Tacurong City. It is designed as a web-based system that can operate offline through a local environment and be accessed through a browser on supported devices.

System features

Everything is connected around the farm workflow.

These features are based on the proposed GameFarm system described in the project document.

01

Smart Farm Planner

Organizes and plans different farm activities so users can see what needs to be done and when.

02

Farm Activity Management

Creates and manages schedules for feeding, health care, conditioning, and other farm activities while helping identify overlapping activities.

03

Breeding, Incubation & Growth

Organizes breeding records, parentage, offspring, bloodline relationships, incubation timelines, reminders, and growth progress.

04

Maintenance & Inventory

Organizes farm and equipment maintenance activities and monitors farm supplies and resources through integrated inventory management.

05

Conflict & Workload Management

Identifies conflicting or overlapping schedules and helps distribute tasks among staff.

06

Farm Dashboard

Displays upcoming tasks, progress, and important farm information for easier review.

07

Reports & Analytics

Provides organized reports for farm activities and records to support review and management.

08

Centralized Farm Information

Keeps farm planning, records, task assignments, development information, and important information within one local platform.

09

Role-Based Access

Authorized users access functions according to their assigned role: Farm Owner/Administrator, Breeder, Trainer, or Farm Staff.

Role-based access

Different users. Different responsibilities.

Each role is given access that matches its responsibilities in the farm workflow.

Administrator

Farm Owner / Administrator

  • Manages overall farm operations
  • Manages users and farm information
  • Reviews schedules, reports, and dashboards
Breeding

Breeder

  • Manages breeding records
  • Views pedigree relationships
  • Plans breeding activities
  • Monitors development information
Conditioning

Trainer

  • Manages conditioning activities
  • Views conditioning schedules
  • Records development-related activities
Operations

Farm Staff

  • Views assigned tasks
  • Follows feeding and maintenance schedules
  • Updates the status of assigned activities
System workflow

From planning to final review.

The workflow connects scheduling, assignments, activity updates, progress monitoring, and review.

01 — Plan

Create and organize farm activities and schedules.

02 — Assign

Check conflicts and workload, then assign tasks.

03 — Perform & Update

Users perform assigned activities and submit status updates.

04 — Monitor & Review

Review dashboards, progress, records, and reports.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Where will GameFarm be used?

The proposed system will be implemented at the SM (Salvador Montilla) GameFarm in Tacurong City as a web-based system that can operate offline through a local environment.

Who can access the system?

Authorized users include the Farm Owner/Administrator, Breeder, Trainer, and Farm Staff, with access based on their assigned roles.

Does the system require internet?

The project document describes GameFarm as a web-based system that can operate offline through a local environment, so internet access is not required for the local operation described in the proposal.

What does role-based access mean?

It means each authorized user works with functions related to their responsibility. For example, the Administrator manages users and farm information, the Breeder handles breeding records, the Trainer handles conditioning activities, and Farm Staff handles assigned operational tasks.

How is my registration information used?

Registration information is stored in the project team's private Google Sheet. Analytics are optional and can be declined without affecting registration.
